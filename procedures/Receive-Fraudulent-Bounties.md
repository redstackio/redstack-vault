# Receive Fraudulent Bounties

## Summary

This procedure details collecting bounty payments from fraudulent submissions, linking them to personal financial accounts.

## Description

Bounties are processed through payment partners and traced back via financial trails, leading to detection.

## Requirements

1. Successful duplicate submissions
2. Linked payment accounts
3. Platform bounty processing

## Defense

Defensive measures and detection strategies:

- Monitor payment trails for anomalies
- Audit bounty claims for duplicates

## Objectives

1. Achieve financial gain
2. Complete the fraud cycle
3. Withdraw funds

## Instructions

### Step 1: Claim Bounty Payments

**Context**: Submit claims under sockpuppet for processed bounties.

> Ensure payments route to personal accounts.

### Step 2: Receive and Withdraw Funds

**Context**: Monitor for deposit and access funds.

> Use traceable financial links.
